Constraints: proposal CL-20261006-001 rev.3; pinned references A Flower Life (brand restraint) and Supply Chain Digital (article architecture).

## Direction contract
seed: none (direction pinned by the signed proposal and user-confirmed palette)
THESIS: A one-person trade paper. Two voices, one masthead: Tina's signed opinion and a dated supply-chain wire. Refuses the marketing landing page of pain points and cards.
OWN-WORLD: Palette sampled from the logo illustration: greige paper, bark ink, olive from Tina's suit, plywood from the wooden letters. Noto Serif TC headlines, Noto Sans TC body. Square corners, hairlines, underlined links, selected state is a square tint block.
STORY: Who Tina is and what she watches, then her latest opinion, then the wire, then answers, then how to hire her.
SIGNATURE: The dateline wire, an olive field where date and source sit in columns beside each headline, repeated in the observation list and article head.
RESTRAINT: Mascot appears once large on the home page. No kickers, no section numbers, no icon cards, no side-stripe borders.
RISK: Opinion list depends on real cover images; two posts have none, so the layout must hold without them.
